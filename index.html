FAQ

Good questions.

Still curious? Call or WhatsApp us at (727) 746-4419 — we reply fast.

Ask on WhatsApp
Do I need to provide water or electricity?

Just let us know when booking whether there's water and power access near your parking spot. If your building has restrictions, tell us in advance and we'll plan around it.

How long does a detail take?

An Express Refresh takes about 1.5–2 hours, a Full Detail about 3–4 hours, and the Showroom Signature can take most of the day depending on paint condition.

Do I have to be home during the service?

No. As long as we can access the vehicle at the start and you're reachable by phone, you can go about your day. We'll send you photos when it's done.

What if it rains?

Florida weather happens. If it rains we'll reschedule at no cost or move to a covered spot like a garage or carport.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes — ¡se habla español! Feel free to call, text or WhatsApp us in English or Spanish.

How do I pay?

Payment is collected after the walk-through when you're 100% happy. Ask us about accepted payment methods when booking.

Can you get rid of pet hair and odors?

Yes — add pet hair removal and odor treatment to any package. Heavy cases may need extra time, and we'll always tell you upfront.
